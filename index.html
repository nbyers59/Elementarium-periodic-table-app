How this app works

Elementarium starts with built-in data, so the table remains useful with no internet. PubChem data is optional and only loads after you choose the button for one element.

  • Tap an element tile to select it.
  • Drag the cube with one finger to rotate it.
  • Use search, category filters, or sorting.
  • PubChem details are saved in your browser after they load.
